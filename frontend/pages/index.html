Reach Out
How does KagoBuild generate house plans?

You describe your home: size, style, rooms, budget and location. Our AI turns that into a floor plan, and you can generate variations until you find one you like.

Do I need architectural experience to use KagoBuild?

No. The design brief uses plain questions and sensible defaults, so you never need architecture knowledge.

How accurate are the material estimates?

Estimates come from your plan and current supplier prices. They are a planning budget, not a contractor's quote, so add 10 to 15 percent for waste and price changes.

Can I design multi-story homes?

Yes. You can set the number of floors in the design brief, and the material list covers every level, including stairs and extra structure.

Is KagoBuild suitable for builders and property developers?

Yes. The Business plan adds team members, supplier quote requests and exportable quotations, so you can price several projects side by side.
