Our Features

AI-powered tools for planning, designing, and budgeting your dream home.

AI-Powered House Design

Generate complete house plans in minutes.

Describe your dream home and let KagoBuild create a customized floor plan tailored to your requirements. Simply specify the number of rooms, building size, architectural style, location, and budget. The AI generates a practical design that helps you move from idea to plan faster than traditional design processes.

Automatic Material Takeoff

Know exactly what materials your project requires.

Once your floor plan is generated, KagoBuild analyzes the structure and calculates the quantities of materials needed for construction. From roofing and flooring to walls, windows, and doors, every major component is included in a detailed material schedule to improve planning accuracy.

Real-Time Cost Estimation

Build with confidence using accurate budgets.

Access current material pricing from trusted suppliers and instantly estimate the total cost of your project. KagoBuild combines your design requirements with supplier pricing data to provide realistic construction budgets before building begins.

Smart Budget Forecasting

Understand the full financial picture of your build.

Beyond material costs, KagoBuild helps forecast project expenses by combining construction requirements, estimated labor costs, and additional building expenses into a single budget overview. This helps homeowners make informed decisions and avoid costly surprises during construction.
