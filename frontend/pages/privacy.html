1. Who we are

KagoBuild ("we", "us") provides AI-assisted house planning, material estimates and supplier quotations. We are the responsible party under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013 (POPIA). Contact: [privacy@yourdomain.co.za], [registered business address], Information Officer: [name].

2. Information we collect

  • Account details: name, email address, password (stored hashed) and phone number if you give it.
  • Project data: design briefs, floor plans, locations, budgets and material lists you create.
  • Usage and device data: pages viewed, browser type, IP address and error logs.
  • Payment details are handled by our payment provider; we do not store full card numbers.
  • Newsletter: your email address, if you subscribe.

3. How we use it

  • To create your account and provide the planner, estimator and quotations.
  • To generate AI house plans and cost estimates from your briefs.
  • To send service messages and, with your consent, marketing emails.
  • To secure the service, prevent fraud and fix problems.
  • To meet legal obligations.

4. Legal basis

We process personal information where it is needed to perform our contract with you, where you consent (for example, newsletters), where the law requires it, or where we have a legitimate interest that does not override your rights.

5. Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us run KagoBuild (hosting, email, payments, analytics and AI processing), with suppliers when you ask us to request a quote, and with authorities where the law requires. We do not sell your personal information.

6. AI processing

Your design briefs may be sent to AI providers to generate plans and estimates. Estimates are planning guides and not professional advice.

7. Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in and, where you allow it, analytics cookies to improve the product. You can block cookies in your browser, but parts of the site may stop working.

8. Retention and security

We keep information while your account is active and for as long as the law requires afterwards. We use encryption in transit, hashed passwords and access controls, but no system is perfectly secure.

9. Transfers outside South Africa

Some providers may store data outside South Africa. We take steps to make sure they protect it to a level comparable to POPIA.

10. Your rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, object to processing, withdraw consent, or unsubscribe at any time. Email us at [privacy@yourdomain.co.za]. You may also complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforeg.org.za.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. If the changes are significant we will tell you by email or on the site.
